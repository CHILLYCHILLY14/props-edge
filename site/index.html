Model & Data Sources

The board separates projection disagreement, no-vig market probability, offered-price value, and wager sizing.

01

Real NFL data only

Live prop prices come from the configured odds providers. ESPN supplies completed regular-season box scores. Preseason is excluded.

02

Early-season restraint

Up to eight recent games feed a recency-weighted, median-stabilized projection. Prior-season influence is reduced until four current-season games exist.

03

Power-method no-vig

Complete Over/Under or Yes/No prices are de-vigged. External books are preferred; DraftKings is the target price.

04

Market-specific probability

Yardage uses a continuous distribution; touchdowns, field goals, interceptions and sacks use count-stat handling with explicit push probability.

05

Fail-closed gates

Thin samples, low confidence, incomplete prices, excessive disagreement, extreme odds, correlated alternates, and exposure excess remain passes.

06

Conservative staking

Suggested stakes use 0.15 Kelly, then scale for confidence and data maturity. PASS rows always receive C$0.
